Traceability test

Pick any risk. See the auditor's question answered link by link.

Every record sits on one chain, from the issue that raised a risk to the evidence that proves its control works. A link that is not ready names the person who holds it and the date it is due.

  • Ready The link exists and its evidence is from this cycle.
  • Pending Assigned to a named person and due before the audit.
  • Broken Overdue, missing, or a required link is absent.

Named accountability

What is still pending, who holds it, and when it is due.

Each management system keeps one list of open work, grouped by the person who holds it.

  • A named person on every pending item

    Each item is held by one person and has a due date. A role on its own never holds work.

  • Reminders sent and logged by the platform

    Remind one holder or every holder at once, by email and in the app. Each reminder is logged.

  • An after-audit flag that follows the audit date

    Items due after the audit date are flagged. When the audit date moves, the flags are recalculated and the holders are told.

Three working areas

Build the system. Run the audit. Keep the certificate.

Implementation

Build and maintain

  • A phase board whose gates hold back work that is not ready
  • Controls and requirements with their status and evidence
  • A document register with owners and review dates

Audit

Run the audit

  • Audit readiness and the traceability test on one screen
  • Pending items per person, each with a due date
  • Findings and corrective actions, verified by someone other than the implementer

Surveillance

Keep the certificate

  • The three-year certification cycle on one timeline
  • Evidence age counted from the date the evidence was produced
  • Items due after the audit date flagged the day they are created

Deployment and data

Your cloud or your data centre. Your files stay where they are.

SaaS and on-premise run the same release with the same features. What differs is who operates it and where the data sits.

SaaS

Operated by CyberLentera

  • A separate database schema for each organisation, with row-level security as a second layer
  • Sign in with your own identity provider
  • Updates in announced maintenance windows

On-premise

Operated by your team

  • Docker Compose on one server, or Kubernetes with Helm
  • Runs in air-gapped networks from a signed offline bundle
  • Licence checked offline, updates on your schedule

Roles per management system

A person can manage the information security system and only read the quality system.

Separation of duties

Nobody verifies their own corrective action or accepts the risk they treated.

Append-only audit trail

Every change is recorded with who made it, what changed and when. Entries are only ever added.

Access for your auditor

Read-only guest access for an external auditor, limited to between 1 and 30 days.
