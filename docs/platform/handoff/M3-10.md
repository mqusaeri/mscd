# M3-10 handoff: Demo personas

M3-10 adds the three demo personas of MILESTONE-3 section E (H.10, accepted by the product owner): Wulan Hartono (Programme Owner), Ratna Kusumawati (Risk Owner) and Gilang Saputra (Compliance Investigator). They are invented people, demo material only (R-13, R-19). They join the three sample organisations (`ndt`, `bsd`, `rmn`) in the server seed, the sample build, the demo Keycloak realm and the persona menu, and they have records of their own so each role shows real work. The seed also writes the routines of Milestone 3 (one open read confirmation request, a retention rule with one due review and one open access review) so the screens of M3-05 to M3-07 have content in the demo. Real users get the new UI roles in the API build too, and the web app shows them the view of the role they stand for.

Audit terms in plain words: a Programme Owner sponsors the management system for the top of the organisation; in MSCD that person verifies and approves work (product decision 6) and approves disposals. A Risk Owner is accountable for a risk and may accept the risk that is left after its treatment (residual risk acceptance, BR-04). A Compliance Investigator follows up reports of possible bribery (a signal) in the anti-bribery management system.
