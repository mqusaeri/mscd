# M2-14 handoff: audit registers screens

M2-14 builds three web screen folders on the screen host of M2-01, in the Audit group of the sidebar: "Internal audit" (the internal audit programme of a certification cycle and its internal audits), "Findings and actions" (findings, corrective actions and the self-assessment) and "Management review". It also adds the "Open findings" panel to the detail of a clause or control in Controls & requirements (detail extension of kind `req`). No server code, shared contract, generated view or shared web file changed.

Audit terms in plain words, as the screens use them: an internal audit is the organisation's own check of its management system against the standard (clause 9.2). A finding is what an audit found: a major or minor nonconformity means a requirement is not met, an opportunity for improvement is advice. A corrective action removes the cause of a nonconformity in steps: correction (fix the problem now), root cause analysis, systemic check (could it happen elsewhere), action (remove the cause), and the effectiveness check by another person. A management review is the meeting where top management looks at the inputs the standard lists and decides what to change (clause 9.3).

Screen `internal-audit`, path `/ms/:ms/internal-audit`, group `audit`, order 40, scope `ms`, perm `ms.read`, personas manager, auditor and top. Page action "Plan internal audit" for `internal_audit.manage` or `audit_event.manage` (MS Managers and Internal Auditors).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Cycle | The open cycle (active, else planned), else the newest, from `ms.cycles.list`; a select when the system has several cycles. Without a cycle: "No certification cycle yet" with where the cycle starts |
| Internal audits | The internal audits of the cycle (`eventType: internal_audit` of the cycle's events): label, date, scope text, report ("Not attached", the record code, or "Attached" for a document the session cannot open). The scope text and report come from `ms.cycles.list` (M2-05 follow-up 8); the report's code and title from the documents of type record of the snapshot, which the scope policy filters |
| Programme coverage | `internal-audit.programme?cycle=`: clauses 4 to 10 and the applicable controls, "Covered" with the audits that covered the row or "Not covered", "n of m covered", and a filter (all, not covered, clauses, controls) |
| Plan internal audit | Name, name in Indonesian (optional, the English name when empty), short label, date, audit days (1 to 365), lead auditor (a member of the organisation, sent as `leadAuditorName`), scope text; `audit-events.create` with `eventType: internal_audit` and an `Idempotency-Key`; the new audit's drawer opens |
| Audit drawer | Date, audit days, lead auditor, scope, report and outcome summary (`readmodel.surveillance.event`); "Attach report" and "Record outcome summary" (`audit-events.update`) for MS Managers and Internal Auditors |
| Audit activities | `audit-activities.list` with "Generate from templates" (`audit-activities.generate`, the number of new activities in the toast; when nothing was created for an audit without activities, the toast says the framework pack has no activity templates for internal audits yet) and "Edit activity": auditor, clause or control (`applicability.targets`), planned date, auditee, status and note (`audit-activities.update`); the key questions and evidence to request of the template show in the dialog. | Attach report | "A registered record": the documents of type record of the system. ## Deviations

1. **The root cause and systemic check texts of a finding are kept by the web app for the browser session.** `FindingItem` does not carry `rcaText`, `systemicCheckText` and `statusNote`, while `FindingPatch` accepts them and BR-06 and BR-07 read them. The API adapter keeps the texts this browser session saved in one module-level map, by organisation and finding, so they stay when the person leaves Findings and actions and comes back, and the Internal audit screen sees them too. A reload of the page, another browser or another person does not see them until the contract carries them; texts the item carries win as soon as it does (follow-up 4).
2. **Key questions and evidence to request are read-only in "Edit activity".** `AuditActivityPatch` (a strict object) carries auditor, target, date, auditee, status and note only, so the dialog shows the template's key questions and evidence to request under "From the template of the framework pack" (follow-up 5).
3. **The lead auditor is stored by name.** `AuditEventCreateRequest` takes `leadAuditorName`, so the form picks a member of the organisation and sends that person's name.
4. **Only an MS Manager makes a new record for the report.** "A new record from a file" registers a document, which needs `document.manage`; an Internal Auditor attaches a registered record.
5. **Inputs of a joint review are recorded from their own system.** `management-reviews.input` writes the definition of the path system, so the drawer offers "Record input" for the inputs of the open system and names the system for the others.
6. **Internal audits of an API build have no activities yet.** No pack holds an audit activity template for internal audits (`packs/sources/*/audit_activities.csv`: ISO/IEC 27001 has three for `stage1` and three for `stage2`, the other five packs none), so "Generate from templates" creates nothing for an internal audit, "Edit activity" has no row to open, and the programme coverage, which counts the activities with a target, stays at "0 of n covered". The screen says so after "Generate from templates" (follow-up 2). The sample build has eight internal audit templates per system, so every part of the screen is tested there.
7. **No page action on a built-in screen.** The three page actions belong to the new screens and are listed in their `meta.json`, so `e2e/shell.spec.js` needs no change for this package.
