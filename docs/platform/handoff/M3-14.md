# M3-14 handoff: Read confirmation, access review and wording fixes

M3-14 fixes the findings of the ISO lead auditor and UU PDP review and of the screen text review of Milestone 3 (2 November 2026) that touch the read confirmation of policies, the periodic access review, the open work totals and the documents for the product owner, the lawyer and the operators. The brief is in `docs/platform/MILESTONE-3.md`, section M3-14; the ids below (ISO M1, texts D1 and so on) are the ids of the review reports. M3-13 ran next to it and owns the erasure, disposal, export and restore fixes; the documents of this package describe M3-13's work from its handoff (`docs/platform/handoff/M3-13.md`).

Audit terms in plain words: a read confirmation is a person's record that they opened an approved policy and confirmed "I have read and understood this document" (ISO/IEC 27001 control A.5.1, clause 7.3); the register is the list of who confirmed what and when, filed as evidence; an access review is the periodic check of who can access what (control A.5.18); a sign-off is a reviewer's recorded approval of their part of the review ("pengesahan" in the Indonesian screens).

## Deviations

1. `AccessReviewComplete` declares `ms` and `evidenceId` optional in zod, so that a missing item gets the rule answer 422 `access_reviews.evidence_required` with its own text; the service requires both. The body carries `ms` next to the item, as the brief's `{ms, evidenceId}` says.
2. `acknowledgement-requests.create` answers 422 `acknowledgements.everyone_confirmed` when everyone in the audience confirmed the version already (the route already listed 422). The brief names only the skip.
3. The day a person was added is the row's `created_at` (reset when an excused row comes back); no column was added, so no migration.
4. The newcomers audit entry carries `newcomers`, `returned` and `date` next to `added` and `reason`.
5. When only one role in a system has a single holder, "Other roles" holds that one person's counts without naming the role, and the API still gives Top Management that role's own row with `holders: 1` (follow-up 1). The note under the table says only what is true in every case: roles held by one person are counted together under "Other roles" and are not named.
6. The operator, legal and plan texts on erasure receipts follow M3-13's deviation 1: the worker signs and emails the receipt within the hour.
7. `apps/web/e2e/api-only/access-review.spec.js` follows the sign-off text of texts D7 ("No evidence item chosen: this system's part of the review report is not filed.").
8. Texts A4 makes "Tenant Admin" the same word in both languages, so the sample check `e2e/i18n.spec.js` ("no host screen label stays in English: users") reports the label of the Invite person dialog. The spec belongs to no package of this wave, so the change is a patch for the orchestrator (need 2).
9. `AccessReviewDetail.evidenceOptions` is not in the brief: the brief asks for the evidence picker of the sign-off form in the Complete dialog, and a Tenant Admin may hold no role in any system. The list covers every system, but titles follow what the caller may read (see the access review table); the codes of the other items stay visible, as the review report already shows the evidence code of each part. A Tenant Admin who files the organisation part to an item they cannot read does not see that item's other files.

## Follow-ups

1. Open work totals, for the product owner (the checks of 2026-11-04 named it twice): when exactly one role of a system has a single holder, "Other roles" holds that one person's counts, and the aggregate answer of `GET /ms/{ms}/audit/pending?aggregate=unit` still gives Top Management each role's own row with `holders`, so whoever reads the answer can tie that role's counts to its one holder. The choices: (a) a lone one-holder role joins "No role or unassigned"; (b) "Other roles" hides its counts while it holds fewer than two people; (c) the server merges the roles with fewer than two holders into one row without a role name before it answers Top Management (a contract change under R-14, outside this package's "that member only"). Each one changes decision 20 ("No role or unassigned" kept as it is) or the contract, so the package keeps the decided behaviour until the owner chooses.
2. Admins without a business role and staff without an MSCD account are still outside the read confirmation audience (second half of ISO m2): it needs an organisation-wide screen permission (M2-16 open item) and a decision on the default audience of H.7. The screen states the gap.
3. A disabled membership keeps the person's business roles (ISO o5): the revoke dialog says so; ending them with the membership changes D.9.
4. GL-69 (disposal receipts) is an open agent row: `check-golive.mjs --agent-items-done` reports it until it is built, which the production go-live needs (decision 20).
5. A person excused because the document became restricted is not asked again when the restriction ends.
6. The human lead auditor and the lawyer review of the updated documents stays open (M3-11 open item).
7. `USER-FLOWS.md` still holds statements from before Milestone 2 that call built screens missing or "API only", mostly in the gap tables of the flows and the first two flows (for example the risk register, the Statement of Applicability, document approval and the management review inputs). This package fixed the ones the reviews named; a full refresh of the document against Milestones 2 and 3 is a separate document task.
