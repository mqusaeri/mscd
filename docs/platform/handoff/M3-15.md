# M3-15 handoff: Small follow-ups

M3-15 closes the small open items of `docs/platform/M3-STATUS.md` and the follow-ups of the M3-10 to M3-14 handoffs that the plan lists under "M3-15 Small follow-ups", and builds product owner decision 21 (H.16) for Open work totals. Disposal receipts (M3-13 follow-up 2, decision 20) stay outside this package.

Audit terms in plain words: the disposal register is the list of records whose keeping period ended, with what was decided and done, which an auditor reads as evidence; a file action says what happened to the file of a disposed record; Open work totals shows Top Management how much work is open per chain stage and business role, without names (decision 2).

## What was built

The persona menu of PT Bank Sentosa Digital and RS Medika Nusantara no longer offers the Compliance Investigator Gilang Saputra, a member without a role there; Nusantara Digital Teknologi still offers all seven personas. The sample-mode unit tests of `canVerify` and `canSeePeople` for the Programme Owner, the Risk Owner and the Compliance Investigator already exist in `apps/web/test/api/controller.test.ts` ("the sample checks of verify and the people list use the base persona of the new personas", M3-10 second review); this package adds the test of `mayActOn` for the seven personas in `apps/web/test/screens/findings.test.tsx`.

### Export build connections (M3-12 minor finding 2)

### Open work totals without one-person numbers (M3-14 follow-up 1, decision 21, H.16)

- `packages/shared/src/api/readmodel.ts`: `PendingAggregate` gains the optional `group: 'other_roles'` (contract change under R-14), and `holders` is described for the merged row too.
- `apps/server/src/modules/read-model-api/screens.ts` (`pendingAggregate`, `withoutOnePersonRows`, `NO_CHAIN_WORK_ROLES`): before the answer of `GET /ms/{ms}/audit/pending?aggregate=unit` leaves the server, every role with fewer than two people besides the reader in the system loses its own row; its counts join one row per stage with `role: null`, `group: 'other_roles'` and `holders` the number of people of all the roles it merges (the reader among them when they hold one). When those roles stand for fewer than two people besides the reader together, their counts join the unassigned row of the stage (`role: null` without `group`). A role whose holders all left (0) merges the same way. Rows come in stage order, then roles in `BUSINESS_ROLES` order, the group row, the unassigned row.
- Who counts as a person behind a row: an active membership and an active account (M3-14 `activeHolders`, unchanged), and a role that can hold chain work. External auditors never count (`NO_CHAIN_WORK_ROLES`), and `rolesByHandle` keeps only `external_auditor` for a guest, as the role resolver does. The reader is left out of the count of people because they read their own open items with `?owner=` of their own handle (deviation 2).
- The counts now come from the system's full chain; the caller's projection no longer limits them (deviation 1).
- Web `pending-totals/`: `keyOfRow` maps the `group` row to "Other roles" and a role row to its role (the client no longer merges by `holders`); `toRows` keeps `group` and refuses any other value or a `group` on a role row; the note under the table, now under every table, says "No number on this screen belongs to one person." with the rule (`otherRolesNote`, `teraHow2`: people besides the reader, external auditors not counted); the sample adapter applies the same rule (`withoutOnePersonRows` and `aggregateItems` of `model.ts`, with the sample persona as the reader through `adapter.ts`), so the sample ISMS shows the Management System Manager, Top Management and the Internal Auditor (one holder each) as one "Other roles" column for three people, two of them besides the reader.

## Deviations

1. **The aggregate counts the full chain.** The answer of `aggregate=unit` was computed from the caller's projected snapshot. The projection of Top Management hides the holder of every item but their own, so for Top Management every other person's open work counted under "No role or unassigned" and only their own items had a role row (the `holders: 1` row of M3-14 follow-up 1 was Top Management's own role). `pendingAggregate` now counts `d.full`, so a reader gets the counts per stage and role a Management System Manager computes from the chain, as D.10 describes. The counts include the open items of records the reader cannot open: restricted documents and evidence, which the projection hides from anyone outside their restricted list (Top Management too, unless they own the record). This is in line with D-04, which computes counts on the full data and shows no record; the answer still names nobody and shows no title or code. The integration tests compare Top Management's answer with the rule computed from the chain items and the grants in the database.
2. **The rule counts the people besides the reader, and external auditors never (major finding of the review; needs the owner's confirmation).** The brief and H.16 say a role with fewer than two holders merges, and a group of fewer than two people joins the unassigned row. Read literally, that still let a row stand for one other person in two setups: (a) Top Management is usually a one-holder role, so with one other one-holder role (for example one Management System Manager) the group was the two of them, and Top Management, who reads their own open items with `?owner=` of their own handle, got the other person's numbers per stage by taking away their own; (b) a guest external auditor counted as one of the group's two people although an auditor holds no chain work, so one manager plus one guest auditor made a group that was exactly the manager's work. To meet the goal of decision 21 ("no answer to Top Management carries a number that belongs to one person"), the rule now counts only the people besides the reader and leaves out holders of `external_auditor` (and every role of a guest but that one). Two readers can get different rows for the same system (each answer is safe for its own reader); the totals per stage are the same. The plan text of H.16 and D.10 needs the same words; the proposed text is below for the orchestrator and the owner.
3. **The group is decided for the whole system.** Which roles merge, and the `holders` of the group row, come from the people of the whole system and are the same in every stage, as a role row's `holders` is. So the group either shows in every stage where its roles hold work, or joins the unassigned row everywhere. A per-stage count would hide more in some stages but would make "Other roles" mean different roles from stage to stage.
4. **The note stands under every table.** When the merged roles join "No role or unassigned", no column points to the rule, so the note shows whenever the table shows. "No role or unassigned" still mixes unassigned items with the work of people who hold no role in the system, as decision 20 keeps it.
5. **The reason of a refused export is one text in two languages.** `organization_export.error` holds one string and the screens show it as it is, so the reason is the English sentences followed by the Indonesian ones. A message key per error would need a schema and screen change outside this package.
6. **One waiting run per organisation is not shown as 409 in the integration test.** The test profile's in-memory job queue drops a duplicate singleton at commit and returns an id, so `test/int/ops/retention-run.test.ts` checks that one run waits; the 409 of `runJob` comes from the pg-boss and BullMQ drivers (unchanged code).
7. **The hook harness moved.** The harness of `findings.test.tsx` (M3-12) is now `apps/web/test/screens/hook-harness.ts`, so `privacy-checks.test.tsx` drives the screen through its hooks too; the findings test imports it. The harness itself is unchanged.
8. **Tests outside the Owns list that state the changed facts (R-15).** `packages/shared/test/enums.test.ts` (the allow-list test reads the M3-15 brief for `retention.review`); `apps/server/test/int/db/tenant-provisioner.test.ts` (`tenant create` reports 12 tenant migrations, 11 before tenant/0012); `apps/server/test/int/parity/snapshots.test.ts` (a known difference: in `bsd` and `rmn` the api's `ROLES`, which is `Me.personas`, leaves out the Compliance Investigator, while the sample has one `ROLES` list for every organisation, as it already had for `PEOPLE`); the specs `e2e/screens/ops.spec.js`, `e2e/api-only/ops.spec.js` (a run of `retention.review`; the API spec runs it for `tst`, which has no due item) and `e2e/personas.spec.js` (the menu of PT Bank Sentosa Digital in the API build). No screen or server file outside the Owns list changed.

7. **For the owner (decision 21, a limit of every column rule):** in a system where only one person besides the reader holds a role with chain work, the total of a stage ("All roles") minus the reader's own items is that person's work, whatever the columns are. In the same way, "No role or unassigned" minus the reader's own items is one person's work when a lone role joins it in a stage that has no unassigned item and no work of a person without a role; H.16 chose that row for the lone roles. Hiding these numbers too would mean showing no totals in small systems. The screen note keeps the sentence the brief asks for; the owner may want it to say that a very small system can still reveal one person's work through its totals.
