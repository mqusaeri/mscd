# M3-03 handoff: end of contract and verified deletion

M3-03 builds D.5 of `docs/platform/MILESTONE-3.md` in full. When a contract ends, the operator moves the organisation to `offboarding`: it stays read-only for `lifecycle.offboarding_days` (30) and its final export is queued. The hourly job `org.lifecycle` then archives it, schedules its deletion `lifecycle.deletion_grace_days` (30) later, reminds the Tenant Admins before each date and starts the purge when it is due. The purge (`org.purge`) runs the ten steps of D.5.2 with the checks of D.5.3, keeps a report that lets a failed purge continue from its failed step, and ends with a signed deletion certificate (D.5.5) emailed to the Tenant Admins captured at scheduling and to the platform operators. Audit rows of the purged organisation are redacted under one attestation entry (D.5.4), so the hash chains and signed anchors still verify. After a restore from a backup older than a purge, `tenant purge --reapply` and `restore.sh --reapply-deletions` delete the organisation again (D.5.6).

## What was built

### Database: `apps/server/migrations/platform/0008_milestone3_purge.sql`

Four functions and the trigger change of D.5.4, nothing else (R-14). All are owned by `mscd_migrator`; `mscd_app` may execute them and keeps no UPDATE or DELETE right on any audit table.

| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| `platform.audit_redact_text(text, jsonb)` | Replaces the listed values (3 characters or more, longest first, case-insensitive, at word bounds) with their pseudonyms |
| `platform.audit_redact_json(jsonb, jsonb)` | The same inside a JSON value, keys included, at any depth |
| `platform.forbid_change()` (replaced) | Still refuses every UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE of `audit_log`, `audit_trail` and the other append-only tables, except one UPDATE of an audit row made by `redact_audit_rows` in the same transaction: the session setting `mscd.audit_redaction` equals the new `redaction_id`, `redacted_at` is set, the address and browser are cleared, and none of `seq`, `id`, `occurred_at`, organisation, system, actor, action, entity, edition, request id, `prev_hash` and `row_hash` changes |
| `platform.redact_audit_rows(chain, seqs, redaction_id, replacements)` (SECURITY DEFINER) | Redacts rows of the platform chain (attestation `tenant.audit_redacted` or `user.audit_redacted`) or of one tenant chain (`privacy.audit_redacted`). Takes the chain lock of `chain_hash`, refuses a reused redaction id, a replacement that is no pseudonym (`erased-<8 hex>` or `Former user <4 to 8 hex>`, so a call can never rewrite other text such as "approved" into "rejected"), a row of another organisation, a row whose recomputed hash differs from its stored `row_hash`, and a redacted row that no longer matches its earlier attestation; any refusal changes nothing. Rows with nothing to remove stay as they are. Appends one attestation entry listing `{seq, rowHash, redactedHash}` of every changed row and returns the counts and the attestation `seq` |
| `platform.purge_organization(org_id)` (SECURITY DEFINER) | Refuses unless the organisation is `archived`, its deletion is `running` and due. Drops the schema; deletes the organisation's rows of `saml_assertion_seen`, `user_identity`, `session`, `auth_transaction`, `identity_provider`, `invitation`, `org_membership`, `verified_domain`, `licence`, `entitlement`, `daily_job_run`, `idempotency_key`, `rate_limit_bucket`, `job_outbox`, waiting `pgboss.job` rows and `tenant_key`; marks open exports `failed` or `expired`. Returns the counts |

### Server

- `modules/audit/audit-redactor.ts` (new, exported by `AuditModule`): `AuditRedactor.redact(trx, {chain, seqs, replacements, attestation, redactionId?})` runs `redact_audit_rows` in the caller's transaction; `platformSeqsOf(trx, orgId)`; `REDACTION_PSEUDONYM`, the two pseudonym forms the database function accepts. Built for M3-04 to reuse: its "Former user" pseudonyms of `erased_person` must have 4 to 8 hex characters.
- `modules/audit/chain-verifier.ts`: the verifier accepts a redacted row only when a later attestation entry of the same chain with the same redaction id lists the row with its stored hash and the hash of its current content; a redacted row changed afterwards breaks the chain at that row. Every result carries `redacted` (0 when the chain holds none). `verifyPlatform` and `verifyTenant` return a `ChainVerification` with two members the route does not show (`publicVerifyResult` strips them): `redactedUnanchored`, the redacted rows whose accepting attestation lies above the newest valid anchor, and `redactionHeadSeq`, the newest accepting attestation. Such rows are counted apart and do not break the chain (see "Anchor coverage of attestations" below). `REDACTION_ATTESTATION_ACTIONS` is exported.
- `modules/audit/audit-trail.service.ts`: every entry carries `redacted` (true or false) and `redactedAt` (or null); the CSV export adds the columns `redacted` and `redacted_at`. `exportCsv` now returns a lazy stream: it runs its first query on the first read, after `FileResponder.send` has written the `file.downloaded` entry (F.13). Before, the pipe started the reading at once, so under load the CSV could leave out its own download entry (an older race, seen as a flaky `test/int/platform/audit.test.ts`). It refuses the system account, platform operators and accounts with another membership. ## Anchor coverage of attestations

D.5.4 wants each attestation entry that accepts a redacted row to be covered by an anchor. The verifier checks it and counts the rows whose attestation lies above the newest valid anchor (`redactedUnanchored`) without marking the chain broken. A broken chain would stop the cover itself: `audit.verify-chain` signs only a chain that verifies, so the next anchor run is what covers a new attestation. Where coverage matters at once, the caller anchors at once and checks it: the purge does so in its `checks` step (the new anchor must reach this purge's attestation and every other accepting attestation), and the anchor job logs `audit.redaction_unanchored` before it signs. M3-04 should anchor the tenant chain after an erasure the same way (`writeAnchor`), or accept that the next nightly run covers it. The route `GET /admin/audit-trail/verify` shows the public members only.

## Follow-ups

1. `keys rewrap` does not rewrap the sealed values of a deletion (`notify_emails_sealed`, the sealed replacement map in the report). They live from scheduling to the end of the purge (about 30 days). If the platform key that sealed them is no longer loaded, the audit step fails until it is (the replacement map) and the certificate email goes to the operators only (the addresses).
2. `cli/admin.ts` lists organisations without leaving out `deleted` ones (not in the Owns list).
3. The Compose files pass no variable for `lifecycle.backup_copies_end_days`; the docs tell the operator to set it in `profiles/saas.yaml`. A `.env` variable would be easier.
4. `deploy/scripts/test/saas-stack.sh` itself still asks for the default IPv6 range of the edge network (`fd6d:7363:6400:1::/64`) unless `MSCD_EDGE_SUBNET6` is set. `restore-test.sh --local` now picks a free range of its own; other callers of `saas-stack.sh` (the SaaS run, the journeys) meet the same refusal while a stopped stack on the host keeps that range. Picking the range in `saas-stack.sh` would fix them all (not in M3-03's Owns list).
5. The vault count of a purge is lost when the process stops between the gateway purge and the write of `vault_objects_deleted` (a moment); a retry then counts 0 and the next backup may need `--allow-vault-deletes`.
6. For M3-04: the pseudonyms of `erased_person` must match `Former user <4 to 8 hex>` (`REDACTION_PSEUDONYM`), or `redact_audit_rows` refuses the call.
