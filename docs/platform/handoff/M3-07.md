# M3-07 handoff: periodic access review

M3-07 builds D.9 in full: the schedule of the periodic access review with `If-Match`, the daily job `access-review.open` with its snapshot, one stored task per system part, the reminders and overdue notices, decisions with `REVIEW-OWN-ACCESS` and the single-reviewer warning `REVIEW-SINGLE-ADMIN`, applying decided revocations through the existing services, the sign-off with the evidence item of control A.5.18 proposed, and the completion with the report in the vault and each system's rows filed as evidence through `fileGeneratedEvidence`. It also builds `admin.users.identities.remove` and the two screens `access-reviews` and `access-review`.

## What was built

### Shared contract (`packages/shared/src/api/access-reviews.ts`, schemas only)

| Schema | Change |
|---|---|
| `AccessReviewItemView` | adds `requestedBy` and `approvedBy` (who asked for a business role and who approved it under the four-eyes flow, D.9; null for the other kinds), `appliedNote` (the note of "Mark as applied"), `pendingApproval` (a business role still waiting for its four-eyes approval at the snapshot) and `needsSecondReviewer` (see below) |
| `AccessReviewPartSummary` (and so `AccessReviewPartDetail`) | adds `needsSecondReviewer` (count), `noReviewer` (an open part of an open review that nobody may decide now), `evidenceCode` and `evidenceFiledAt` |
| `AccessReviewDetail` | adds `warnings` (the warnings the review completed with, or that completing it now would give) |

Examples follow the new members. No route, permission, enum or rule id changed. `admin-users.ts` needed no change.

### Server

`apps/server/src/modules/access-reviews/` (module README lists the files):

- Settings (`access-reviews.settings.get` and `.put`): one row per organisation; without a row the read answers the H.9 defaults (every 3 months, 14 days, no next due date) with the ETag `W/"0"`, which the first PUT takes as `If-Match`. The next due date is today or later (400 on `body.nextDueOn`); audited `access_review.settings_changed` with before and after.
- Opening (`access-review-open.service.ts`): by the daily job on the due date, or by a Tenant Admin with "Start review now" (`access-reviews.create`, 409 with "already open" while one is open, besides the partial unique index). The review number is `AR-<year>-<nn>`; the parts are the organisation part and one part per system that is not archived; the snapshot (`access-review-snapshot.ts`) takes the item kinds of D.9; one stored task per system part (source `access_review`, entity `access_review_part`, entity code the review number) for the system's primary Management System Manager, else its first one; the notice `access_review_due` to the Tenant Admins and to the Management System Managers and Programme Owners of each system part; the next due date moves on by the frequency (from the due date for the schedule, past every date already gone; from today for "Start review now"). Audited `access_review.opened`.
- Daily job (`access-review-open.job.ts`, `daily`): an organisation without a settings row gets one with the first review due one period later; a review opens when the due date has come and none is open; an open review sends `access_review_due` three days before its due date and `access_review_overdue` the day after it and every 7 days after that, to the reviewers whose part is not signed off and to the Tenant Admins. The dates sent are kept in `access_review.summary.reminders`, so a repeated run of a day sends nothing twice.
- Decisions (`access-reviews.decide` for organisation items, `access-reviews.ms.decide` for the items of a system): keep, change (with a note, the shared schema refuses a change without one) or revoke; `REVIEW-OWN-ACCESS` (422 `business-rule`, `messageKey` `rule.REVIEW-OWN-ACCESS`) on one's own access; 409 once the part is signed off, the decision applied or explained, or the review no longer open. Audited `access_review.item_decided`.
- Applying (`access-review-apply.service.ts`): the table of D.9. `access-reviews.apply` (`user.manage`) applies `business_role`, `admin_role`, `membership` and `linked_sign_in` revocations through `RoleGrantsService.revoke`, `UsersService.update` and `IdentitiesService.remove`; `access-reviews.ms.apply` (`auditor_access.grant`) revokes guest grants through `AuditorAccessService.revoke`. Each route refuses the other route's items with 409. Both take "Mark as applied" (a change, or a revocation made elsewhere, with an optional note) and "Not applied" (a reason). A right that is gone already is recorded as `already_removed`. Audited `access_review.item_applied` beside the event of the service.
- Sign-off (`access-reviews.ms.sign-off`): when every item is decided (except items that need a second reviewer); the evidence item must belong to the system (404 otherwise); the stored task of the part is closed `done`. `access-reviews.ms.get` proposes, for a system on an ISO/IEC 27001 pack, the evidence item whose targets include control `A.5.18` (`suggestedEvidence`). Audited `access_review.signed_off`.
- Completion (`access-reviews.complete`): when every system part is signed off, every organisation item decided (same exception) and every revocation or change applied or explained; 409 names what waits, and names a system that nobody may review on its own ("ISMS has no Management System Manager or Programme Owner to review it; assign one on People and roles, or cancel the review"). The organisation part is signed off by the completer, the report CSV is stored in the organisation's first Managed Vault (`access-reviews/<review id>`, `access_review.report_object_id`; skipped when there is none, the report route still answers), and each system's rows are filed as a new file of the evidence item named at its sign-off, in the same transaction. The summary keeps the counts, the files filed and the warnings. Audited `access_review.completed`.
- Cancel with a reason closes the open tasks `cancelled`; the report (`access-reviews.report`) is a formula-safe CSV (F.15) audited `file.downloaded`. Its columns include `granted_by` and, for a business role, `requested_by` and `approved_by` (the four-eyes record of D.9), so the file filed as A.5.18 evidence keeps who asked for and who approved each role.

`apps/server/src/modules/admin-users/identities.service.ts` and the route `admin.users.identities.remove` in `admin-users.controller.ts`: removes the `platform.user_identity` rows of a member for one of this organisation's providers (by slug), ends that person's sessions signed in through that provider (`revoke_reason` `identity_unlinked`) and audits `identity.unlinked` in the organisation's platform log with the provider, the subjects, the reason and the sessions ended. 404 for a non-member, a provider of another organisation and a member without a link. `admin-users.module.ts` provides the service and exports `UsersService`, `RoleGrantsService` and `IdentitiesService`.

### Web

The shared model, both adapters, the change counter and the shared parts (item table, decision form, apply forms) live here and are used by the second screen. The "Granted by" column of the item table names, for a business role, who asked for it and who approved it, with the grant date.
- `apps/web/src/screens/access-review/` (Audit, `access_review.decide`): the system's part of the open review (or an earlier one through "Review"), its items with filters and selection, "Revoke guest access" for a guest grant decided "revoke" (only with `auditor_access.grant`), "Mark as applied" and "Not applied" for guest grants, and "Sign off" with the evidence item picker (the A.5.18 item proposed, "Choose none" to file nothing).
- The sample build holds an open review opened by the schedule five days ago (tono decided rina's organisation items) and a completed one of the last quarter, from the people and grants of People and roles, a linked company sign-in for bagus and a guest auditor in the ISMS.

## Deviations

1. "Needs a second reviewer" covers the system parts too. D.9 names the single Tenant Admin only, but the Test Organisation's ISMS has one reviewer (rina), and the plan's API run has rina sign off with her own role undecided. An undecided item whose subject is the only holder of the deciding right of its part (Tenant Admins for the organisation part, Management System Managers and Programme Owners for a system part) does not hold up the sign-off or the completion; the review completes with `REVIEW-SINGLE-ADMIN`, whose text names the single Tenant Admin case with the rule's message, adds one sentence per system with a single reviewer, and lists the people. The reviewers are read when the review is read, so a reviewer added during the review can still decide the item. A part that nobody may decide (a system without an active Management System Manager or Programme Owner) is its own case: its items do not need a second reviewer, the part shows `noReviewer`, it holds up the completion with its own message, and the Tenant Admin assigns a reviewer or cancels the review. A separate warning id for the system case (for example `REVIEW-SINGLE-REVIEWER`) would need a contract correction (R-14); I reused the existing id.
2. The guest auditor grant's own `user_ms_role` row (External Auditor) is reviewed once, as the `auditor_access` item; `business_role` items leave out role rows that a guest grant points to. Guest accounts' memberships are left out of the `membership` items for the same reason. External Auditor roles granted to members through People and roles stay `business_role` items with their end date.
3. Memberships: `apply` on an invited membership answers 409 (no service withdraws an invitation, and `UsersService.update` refuses to disable it); the Tenant Admin records it as not applied or marks it applied once the invitation lapsed.
4. Applying runs the service of the right in its own transaction (it rotates the caller's session when the caller's own roles change) and marks the item in the next; a repeated request finds the right gone and records `already_removed`.
5. A failed evidence filing refuses the whole completion with the problem of `fileGeneratedEvidence` (for example a repository that takes no server uploads), so nothing is filed half; it completes once the routing is fixed.
6. The last sign-in of an item is the latest session the person started in this organisation (`platform.session`). The account's global `last_login_at` would show activity in other organisations (F.6), so the review does not use it. For a guest without a session it is the last use of the grant.
7. The organisation without a settings row: the first daily run writes the H.9 defaults with the first review due one period later, so every organisation reviews access without setup; the settings read shows `nextDueOn: null` until then.
8. Schema additions in my area file (above): optional fields were not needed; the new members are required and filled by the server.
9. Files outside the Owns list: none changed. `apps/web/test/api/controller.test.ts` and `apps/web/test/screens/host.test.tsx` (the orchestrator's) list the exact Administration entries of the sample manager and now fail on the new "Access reviews" entry; the patch is below.
