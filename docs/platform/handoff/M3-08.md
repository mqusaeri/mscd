# M3-08 handoff: Open work totals

M3-08 builds the host screen `pending-totals` (MILESTONE-3 D.10, C.2): Top Management, the Programme Owner and the Management System Manager see how much work is open and overdue along the chain of a management system, per chain stage and per business role, with no name, holder or due date (product decision 2). The screen reads the existing route `GET /api/v1/ms/:ms/audit/pending?aggregate=unit` (`readmodel.audit.pending`, permission `audit.pending.aggregate` [MM, PO, TM]). No server code, shared contract, generated view or shared web file changed (D3-11).

Audit terms in plain words: the chain is the line an auditor follows from the context of the organisation through risks, treatments, controls, documents and evidence to metrics (the stages of "Chain stages"). An open item is a record of the chain that still waits for someone's action; it is overdue when its due date has passed, or missing when it has neither a holder nor a due date, or when its source file is gone ("Overdue / missing" on Chain stages). An item with a holder but no due date is still on time. The business role is the role a person holds in the system (for example Domain Owner); the screen counts each item under the role of its holder and never shows who that is.

## Deviations

1. **No sidebar entry.** D3-02 says the totals are "reached through page actions on the built-in screens", and D.10 that "the sample build reaches the screen through its address", so the screen has `nav: false` and `meta.json` `open: query` with `sampleRole: top`. The probe, contrast and i18n specs open it with `?screen=pending-totals&persona=top`; the API display walks do not visit it, because they follow the sidebar.
2. **"No role or unassigned" is one column.** The api answers `role: null` both for unassigned items and for holders without a role in the system, so the screen cannot split them.
3. **Every stage is listed.** The table shows each stage of the system's chain (`STAGES[ms]`), also those without open work, so the reader sees the whole chain; a stage the answer names that the snapshot does not list goes last, labelled by its key.
4. **The acceptance check of `apps/web/e2e/helpers.js` reports a change that is not this package's.** `git diff --quiet HEAD -- apps/web/src/MscdApp.jsx apps/web/src/styles.css apps/web/e2e/helpers.js` fails because M2-21 (which owns `helpers.js`) has uncommitted changes to it in this working tree. M3-08 did not touch `helpers.js`; `MscdApp.jsx` and `styles.css` are unchanged (checked separately). The two specs import only helpers that `helpers.js` exports at `HEAD` (`test`, `expect`, `ui`, `go`, `openApp`, `openScreen`, `metaById`, `expectReadableText`, `expectNoHorizontalOverflow`, `expectNoTextOverflow`); they do not use M2-21's new `pageSettled` and wait for a built-in page action ("Remind all holders", "Readiness report") before they check the page action list instead, so M3-08 can be committed before, with or after M2-21. Once M2-21 is committed, the full check passes.
5. **The overdue label uses the words of Chain stages.** The count and the column of overdue items read "Overdue / missing" ("Terlambat / hilang"), as on Chain stages (`data/static.js`), and the sentences that explain it use the same Indonesian words ("terlambat atau hilang"), so a reader who compares the two screens finds one name for one number (product decision 10).
