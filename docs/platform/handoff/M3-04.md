# M3-04 handoff: requests from one person

M3-04 builds D.6 in full: the register of personal data requests with the 72-hour due time and its reminders, the system checks with the mention search, the action of each request type (copy of the data, correction, stop to the use, erasure under a pseudonym), "My data" in My account, the pseudonyms everywhere a person is named after an erasure, the personal data export, and the retention of sessions, tokens and read notifications of D.6.7. It also fills the `acknowledgement` member of the document record in the snapshot (D.7). The screens are `privacy-requests` and `privacy-checks`, plus the "My data" section of `account`.

## What was built

### Shared contract

No change. The schemas of `packages/shared/src/api/privacy.ts` and `exports.ts` written by M3-01 carry everything the routes need. The rate limits of M3-01 stay as they were.

### Server

`apps/server/src/modules/privacy/`:

- `privacy-requests.service.ts` and `privacy-requests.controller.ts`: the register (`privacy-requests.list`, filters by status, type and search text, open requests first by due time), recording (`privacy-requests.create`, a member by user id or a person without an account by name and email; a receive time more than 5 minutes in the future is refused; due at receive time plus `privacy.request_due_hours`), one request with its checks, the steps taken (from the audit trail), the latest note and the task of a queued copy (`privacy-requests.get`), and the moves with `If-Match` (`privacy-requests.update`: start work with the identity check note, refuse or record a withdrawal with a reason, notes). Recording a copy or an erasure request opens one `privacy_check` per system of the organisation, with a stored task (source `privacy_check`) and the notice `privacy_check_requested` for the primary Management System Manager. A request from My account tells the Tenant Admins (`privacy_request_received`). The audit entries of a request never hold the person's name or email.
- Correction (`privacy-requests.rectify`): display name and job title of an account that belongs to this organisation alone; 422 `shared_account` (a business rule, J.6) when the account has another active membership (the person corrects it in My account). Audited `user.updated` and `privacy_request.rectified`.
- Stop to the use (`privacy-requests.restrict`): the membership is disabled and the person's sessions in the organisation end. Refusals in this order: already restricted (409), last active Tenant Admin (409 `last_tenant_admin`), one's own membership (403).
- `privacy-erasure.service.ts` (`privacy-requests.erase`, D.6.5): every check done (409 `privacy.checks_open`), no open items (422 `ERASE-OPEN-ITEMS` from the holdings of Handover, without the person's own tasks of these screens), not the last active Tenant Admin, not the caller. Then in one transaction: business roles revoked (`role.revoked` with the pseudonym), this organisation's linked sign-ins and invitations removed, membership removed, sessions ended (`account_erased`), the `erased_person` row with a new pseudonym ("Former user" and 4 to 8 hex characters), pending read confirmations excused, open tasks cancelled, notifications and calendar feed tokens deleted, the personal data exports about the person withdrawn (a queued build fails; a built file expires at once and is marked deleted, so it no longer downloads and the next `cleanup.expired` deletes its vault content; its name, its stored object and its task result take the handle form of the pseudonym; the `export_ready` notice to whoever asked for it is deleted), the person's values removed from the tenant and platform audit rows by attested redaction (`AuditRedactor`), and the account anonymised through `AccountAnonymiser` when the person has no other membership. A person without an account is recorded as erased once every check is done. The result counts each step; audited `privacy_request.erased`.
- Closing (`privacy-requests.close`): needs work started, every check done for a copy or an erasure, and the action of the type (a copy queued, the person erased, the membership disabled); a correction closes after the account or the records were corrected.
- `privacy-checks.service.ts` and `privacy-checks.controller.ts` (`privacy_request.assist`): the checks of the system (open first by due time, then done ones), the mentions (`privacy-checks.mentions`) and "Mark checked" with a note, which completes the manager's stored task (audited `privacy_check.done`).
- `mention-columns.ts` and `mention-search.ts`: the searched text columns of 44 tables (titles, descriptions, notes, justifications, `audit_event.lead_auditor_name`, `storage_object.provider_modified_by`, the texts of interested parties and business associates) with the scope rule of each table (system, link to the system, parent record, stored object, evidence), and `NOT_SEARCHED`, the tables and columns left out, each with its reason (codes, status values, the audit trail, the request itself, settings, secrets, transient rows, organisation-wide rows, guests, ratings). The search matches the person's name, email and account handle as whole words, case-insensitive, at most 500 results with record kind, code, field and an excerpt of up to 300 characters.
- `me-privacy.service.ts` and `me-privacy.controller.ts`: the person's own requests, a new request from My account (correction, restriction or erasure; 409 `privacy.open_request` while one of the same type is open; channel `self_service`), and `me.profile` (204; audited `user.updated` with `source: my_account`).
- `person-export.service.ts` and `person-export.controller.ts`: `exports.person.create` (an access request in progress about a member) and `exports.me.create` queue an export of kind `personal_data` through `ExportsService.queuePersonalData`. `exports.person.create` refuses a person erased in this organisation (409 `privacy.already_erased`).
- `privacy-deadlines.job.ts` (`privacy.deadlines`, tenant scope, daily at 08:00 local): each run tells the Tenant Admins about every open request due within the next 24 hours ("due soon") and every open request past its due time ("overdue"). The overdue notice repeats on each daily run until the request is answered; nothing deduplicates it.
- `privacy-text.ts` and `privacy-model.ts`: the texts of notices and refusals in both languages, the request rows and numbers (`PR-<year>-<nnn>`, under an advisory lock), the pseudonym draws.
- `pseudonym.ts`: the shared helpers for every query that names a person for an organisation: `erasedJoin`, `shownHandle`, `shownName`, `erasedValue`, the scalar subqueries `handleOfUser` and `nameOfUser`, `pseudonymHandle`, `ERASED_JOB_TITLE` and `erasedPeople`. The privacy module, the read model loader, the audit trail, the user list and the exports use them, and the patches below use them for the files of other packages.

Other modules:

- `exports/export-content.service.ts` and `exports/calendar-feed.service.ts`: the SoA, document register and readiness exports and the review calendar feed name an erased person by the pseudonym ("Former user 7F3A"): their names map holds the organisation's `erased_person` rows under the handle form that the read model gives.
- `exports/personal-data.ts`, `exports.service.ts`, `export-build.job.ts`: the personal data export (`mscd-personal-data-v1`, JSON): account, membership, business roles, linked sign-ins, sessions, sign-in events, audit entries about the person, the records the person holds (found through the holder columns of the schema), read confirmations, competence records, notifications and requests. The file is `personal-data-<handle>-<date>.json`; it downloads for the person (own export) or a Tenant Admin with `privacy_request.manage`. `export.build` fails without a retry for a person erased in the organisation: it checks before the build and again in step 4 after it locks the task row, which the erasure locks too, so an erasure that runs meanwhile either withdraws the finished file or makes the build fail.
- `jobs/cleanup-expired.job.ts`: ended sessions after `privacy.session_retention_days`, used, revoked or expired invitations and password tokens after `privacy.token_retention_days`, read notifications after `privacy.notification_retention_days` (except those a reminder refers to). The result counts each kind.
- `read-model/`: a person erased in the organisation shows under the handle form of the pseudonym (`former-user-7f3a`) with the pseudonym as name and "Former member" as job title, also after the role filter of the snapshot (the loader's `handleOf` is `handleOfUser`); the document record carries `acknowledgement` when its settings differ from the defaults.
- `audit/audit-trail.service.ts` and `admin-users/user-views.ts`: the pseudonym in place of the actor's handle and name, and of the member's name.

## Deviations and decisions

1. Handle form of a pseudonym. `PersonId` allows no spaces, so where a handle is expected (snapshot people, audit trail actor, request subject) an erased person shows as `former-user-7f3a`, with "Former user 7F3A" as the name.
2. The snapshot carries `acknowledgement` only for settings that differ from the defaults, so the snapshots of documents nobody configured stay as they were (parity of J.4). A document record without the member has the default settings. The web side of M3-05 still reads a missing member as "unknown"; the patch below changes that.
3. A copy of the data exists only for a member. For a person without an account the drawer says to collect what the system checks found and send it to the person.
4. The test stack of the API E2E suite runs no worker, so a queued copy stays "being prepared" there; the integration tests build and download it.
5. The open request is not part of the screen address (M2-20 keeps `ms`, `org`, `tab` and `persona`); notices open it through `params.request`.
6. Mentions of record kinds without a screen of their own (tasks, audit activities, continuity plans and others) show "Edit on its page" instead of a link.
7. A person erased and later invited again. The erasure keeps the `erased_person` row, and `invitation.service.ts` and `users.service.ts` revive a removed membership with `deleted_at: null`. Such a person then shows in People and roles as "Former user 7F3A" next to their real email, and every person column of the snapshot names them by the pseudonym, also for records they own after rejoining. Question for the orchestrator: should an invitation of a person erased in the organisation be refused with a message (owner of `identity/invitation.service.ts` and `admin-users/users.service.ts`), or should rejoining end the pseudonym for new work? M3-04 leaves the behaviour as it is until this is decided.
8. The texts "Record a personal data request" and "Ask the organisation" no longer name 72 hours: the server takes the due time from the profile key `privacy.request_due_hours` (H.4: the lawyer confirms it per type), and the list shows the due time once the request is recorded.
